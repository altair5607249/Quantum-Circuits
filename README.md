# Quantum-Circuits
This contains all of my experimentation with quantum circuits, aimed to better my understanding in the probabilistic nature of quantum systems.

## Note
From the moment I watched AoButa, I became interested with the weirdness of quantum mechanics. Bit by bit, I learned more about it, whether it was through other popular media than AoButa or the occasional Google search.

That interest grew into fixation when I was offered to join a dual enrollment program in quantum computing by one of my teachers, hosted by Frontier Technology Institute. I didn't think it would too interesting at first, but as I write this and it being my fourth week taking the class, I can safely say that my expectations have been defied.

During one of our classes, Google Cirq was recommended to us, so I decided to play around with it and found it to be a really fun way for me to learn more about quantum mechanics in general. From then on, my experimentation began, and it's been a blast, so I've decided to document it here!
