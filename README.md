# Quantum-Circuits
This contains all of my experimentation with quantum circuits, aimed to better my understanding of the probabilistic nature of quantum systems.

## How It Began
From the moment I watched [AoButa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rascal_Does_Not_Dream) (Rascal Does Not Dream of Bunny Girl Senpai), I became interested with the weirdness of quantum mechanics. Bit by bit, I learned more about it, whether it was through other popular media than AoButa or the occasional Google search.

That interest grew into fixation when I was offered to join a dual enrollment program in quantum computing by one of my teachers, hosted by Frontier Technology Institute. I didn't think it would too interesting at first, but as I write this in retrospect, I can safely say that my expectations have been defied.

During one of our classes, [Google Cirq](https://quantumai.google/cirq) was recommended to us, so I decided to play around with it and found it to be a really fun way for me to learn more about quantum mechanics in general. From then on, my experimentation began, and it's been a blast since then, so I've decided to document it all here as I continue my experiments!
